The Government of India and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced a 20,000 crore government securities (G-Sec) buyback auction on July 28, 2026.

A bond buyback is a transaction where the government purchases its own outstanding bonds back from investors before their scheduled maturity date. In an RBI G-Sec buyback, the RBI pays cash directly to the bondholders rather than swapping them for other securities.

The purchased bonds are immediately extinguished, permanently reducing the government's outstanding debt. And because the government is transferring cash from its accounts into the banking system, a successful buyback injects durable rupee liquidity into the market.

The targeted securities in this buyback are - 7.33% GS 2026, 5.74% GS 2026,
8.15% GS 2026, and 8.24% GS 2027.
