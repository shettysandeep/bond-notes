Banks are currently tapping certificate of deposits (CDs) for short term fund
raising and manage liquidity. The CD rates were much higher. 2-3 months tenure
was around 6.5-7 percent and 1-year was around 7 percent.([source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-borrowing-costs-rise-in-january-as-cd-rates-cross-7/127669133))
The top four by amount raised are : SIDBI (7500 cr), SBI (6740 cr), NABARD (5750 cr), and HDFC Bank (5700 cr). [source](https://nsdl.com/nsdl/2026-07/Certificate_of_Deposit_Issuance_in_the_month_of_January_2026.html)

The tenure varied across banks. SBI's tenure was 77 days while the others in the
list above were for a year.

Liquidity in the system fluctuates with the changes in demand. Dollar
outflows are definitely straining liquidity as RBI remains active in supporting the rupee.
