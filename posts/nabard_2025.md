The bond markets are in a situation today. NABARD cancelled its short term debt the second time as the as the asking yield was high. Bids for NABARD's 3-year bond came in between 6.84% and 7.12% compared to the expectation
between 6.70% and 6.75%.

NABARD had previously scrapped the same issue in late November 2025. Power Finance Corporation (PFC) also pulled ₹6,000 crore in planned bond issuances the previous week for the exact same reason.

This is despite the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) cutting the repo rate to 5.25% and announcing liquidity
injections earlier in December 2025. As of now liquidity remains tight.
