The State Government (except Sikkim) can obtain Ways and Means Advances (WMA), which
are temporary advances provided by the RBI, to help in managing short-term mismatches
between their receipts and payments. During FY 2024-25, 16 States availed WMA including
overdraft of
6.87 lakh crore out of which three States namely Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh and
Telangana availed 62 percent of the total WMA including overdraft. The duration of
WMA/overdraft usage was 365 days in Punjab, 363 days in Telangana, 357 days in Andhra
Pradesh, 325 days in Kerala and 271 days in Rajasthan, suggesting near-continuous
dependence throughout the year. 11 States did not avail of any WMA in FY 2024-25.
